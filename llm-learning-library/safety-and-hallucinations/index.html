Risk and reliability

Safety and Hallucinations

LLMs are persuasive generators, not guaranteed truth engines. Hallucinations, prompt injection, unsafe actions, and data leakage are all system-level risks.

Framing

Safety is not one filter. It is a layered design problem across prompts, retrieval, tools, permissions, and evaluation.

Why hallucinations happen

The model is optimized to produce plausible continuations. If evidence is missing or weak, it may still answer confidently. That is why factual tasks often need grounding and verification.

Fluent wrong answers

Style and correctness are not the same.

Task dependence

Some tasks are inherently safer than others.

Mitigation layers

  • Ground with retrieval or tools.
  • Constrain actions with validation and approvals.
  • Separate trusted instructions from untrusted retrieved text.
  • Use adversarial evals, not just happy-path tests.
