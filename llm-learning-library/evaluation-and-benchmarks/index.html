Measurement

Evaluation and Benchmarks

You cannot improve what you do not measure. Public benchmarks help compare models broadly, but product quality depends on your own task-specific evaluation set.

Operating principle

If you cannot evaluate a change, you cannot safely optimize it.

What to evaluate

Task success

Did the system solve the user’s actual problem?

Faithfulness

Did it stay grounded in evidence or truth labels?

Robustness

Did it handle ambiguity, noise, and edge cases?

Cost

Was the result economically viable?

Latency

Was it responsive enough for the use case?

Safety

Did policy and failure behavior remain acceptable?

Benchmark caution

Benchmarks are easy to overread. A model can rank highly on a public leaderboard yet underperform badly on your document corpus, workflow, tool schemas, or domain language.

Evaluation turns demo impressions into engineering evidence.
