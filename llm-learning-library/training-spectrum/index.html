Training landscape

Pretraining, Training, and Fine-Tuning

Modern LLMs usually go through multiple stages: broad pretraining, chat or instruction tuning, preference or alignment tuning, and sometimes narrow task-specific fine-tuning.

Important distinction

Prompting changes inputs. RAG changes runtime evidence. Fine-tuning changes weights.

The phases

1

Pretraining

Massive next-token prediction over broad corpora.

2

Instruction tuning

Teach the model to behave usefully in chat-like tasks.

3

Preference / alignment tuning

Shape behavior toward chosen safety and helpfulness goals.

4

Task-specific fine-tuning

Adapt the model to narrower behaviors or domains.
